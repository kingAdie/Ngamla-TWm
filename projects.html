Project Description

Ngamla Twm Security and Surveillance Solutions successfully completed the installation of a comprehensive CCTV surveillance system at the National Library of South Africa in Cape Town — one of the country's most significant public institutions.

The scope of work included full system design, professional camera installation across multiple zones, NVR configuration, cable management, and comprehensive commissioning — all delivered with minimal disruption to library operations.

The project demonstrates our capability to plan, execute, and commission large-scale surveillance infrastructure for major government and public-sector clients to the highest standards.

Scope of Work

01
System Design

Customised CCTV system design based on the library's layout, security requirements, and operational needs.

02
Camera Installation

Professional installation of HD cameras at strategic locations throughout the facility, covering all critical areas.

03
NVR Configuration

Full NVR setup, camera integration, storage configuration, and remote access enabling for the client's monitoring team.

04
Testing & Commissioning

Comprehensive testing, full system commissioning, client handover, and documentation of the installed infrastructure.

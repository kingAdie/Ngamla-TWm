About Ngamla Twm

A PSIRA-registered, government-approved security and surveillance company delivering professional protection for government, institutional, and commercial clients across South Africa.

Company Overview

Built on Trust & Compliance

Ngamla Twm Security and Surveillance Solutions is a fully registered South African security services provider specialising in manned guarding, CCTV surveillance, alarm systems, access control, mobile patrol, and event security.

The company delivers structured, compliant, and reliable security solutions tailored to the specific requirements of government institutions, public facilities, and commercial entities across South Africa.

We are committed to supporting crime prevention, asset protection, and operational safety through professional security deployment and modern surveillance technology — aligned with all regulatory and industry standards.
